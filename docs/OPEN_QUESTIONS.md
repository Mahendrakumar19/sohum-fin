# Open Questions and Client Decisions

Resolve these before final design approval and production launch.

## 1. Company identity

-   [ ] Confirm exact registered legal name and preferred display name.
-   [ ] Provide official logo in SVG or transparent PNG.
-   [ ] Confirm brand colors, fonts, and brand usage rules.
-   [ ] Confirm official website domain and preferred hostname.
-   [ ] Confirm registered/business address and whether it should be
    public.

## 2. Contact information

-   [ ] Verify phone number shown in the screenshot.
-   [ ] Verify email shown in the screenshot.
-   [ ] Provide official WhatsApp number and approved prefilled message.
-   [ ] Provide social media URLs.
-   [ ] Confirm business hours, if they should be displayed.

## 3. Services and compliance

-   [ ] Confirm which investment, stock, and insurance services are
    actually offered.
-   [ ] Clarify the company's role for each service and the wording that
    may legally be used.
-   [ ] Confirm DSA status and which loan products can be promoted.
-   [ ] Confirm whether any regulatory registration/disclosure must
    appear.
-   [ ] Confirm that claims such as "expert guidance," "quick
    processing," or "transparent" are substantiated.
-   [ ] Obtain approved risk disclosures and disclaimers.

## 4. Banking and NBFC partners

-   [ ] Verify every named bank/NBFC relationship.
-   [ ] Obtain permission or brand assets for each logo.
-   [ ] Confirm the final list and display order.
-   [ ] Remove any partner that is no longer current or cannot be
    represented publicly.

## 5. Content and design

-   [ ] Approve hero headline and supporting copy.
-   [ ] Provide original photographs or approve licensed imagery.
-   [ ] Decide whether the homepage is a single landing page or a
    multi-page website.
-   [ ] Confirm whether each loan type needs a dedicated SEO page.
-   [ ] Confirm English-only launch or Hindi/other languages.
-   [ ] Provide testimonials only if genuine, permissioned, and legally
    appropriate.

## 6. Enquiry handling

-   [ ] Where should enquiries go: email, WhatsApp, CRM, or database?
-   [ ] Is a backend/database required, or is a third-party form service
    acceptable?
-   [ ] Which fields are necessary?
-   [ ] Who can access enquiries, and how long should they be retained?
-   [ ] Is an admin dashboard needed now or in a future phase?
-   [ ] Who owns the transactional email/service accounts?

## 7. Analytics and SEO

-   [ ] Is Google Analytics or another analytics tool required?
-   [ ] Who owns Google Search Console?
-   [ ] Which service areas and search terms matter?
-   [ ] Are there existing URLs that need redirects?
-   [ ] Is ongoing SEO/content work in scope?

## 8. Hosting and delivery

-   [ ] Who owns the domain and hosting?
-   [ ] Select Vercel or VPS deployment.
-   [ ] Who provides DNS access?
-   [ ] Who pays recurring costs?
-   [ ] Confirm final timeline and approval contact.
-   [ ] Confirm GST treatment, payment milestones, warranty, and
    maintenance terms.

## Launch blocker

Do not publish unverified contact details, partner logos, regulatory
claims, guaranteed rates/returns, or unsupported financial-service
promises.

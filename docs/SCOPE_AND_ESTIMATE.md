# Scope, Estimate, and Delivery Plan

## 1. Proposed base scope

-   Responsive marketing website inspired by the client-provided
    reference.
-   Homepage with hero and trust highlights.
-   Investment section for Mutual Funds, Stocks, and Insurance.
-   Loans section for seven loan categories.
-   Functional EMI calculator.
-   About and Contact content.
-   Contact methods and a basic enquiry form.
-   Footer, legal links, and approved partner section.
-   Basic on-page SEO, performance optimization, responsive QA, and
    deployment.

The final route/page count must be confirmed before development.
Dedicated SEO pages for every service may change the effort and
estimate.

## 2. Indicative development estimate

  UI/UX design and visual styling                   ₹12,000

This is a planning estimate, not a binding offer. Tax treatment and
invoicing must be confirmed by the service provider/accountant.

## 3. Suggested payment milestones

-   40% advance: ₹22,000
-   40% after design and staging preview approval: ₹22,000
-   20% before production handover: ₹11,000

GST, if applicable, is additional to these pre-tax milestones.

## 4. Timeline

Indicative delivery: 10--15 working days after receipt of the advance,
assets, verified content, and required access.

Suggested phases: - Days 1--2: discovery, content confirmation, sitemap,
wireframes. - Days 3--5: visual design and client review. - Days 6--10:
implementation and responsive behavior. - Days 11--12: calculator,
forms, SEO, and integrations. - Days 13--15: QA, revisions within scope,
deployment, and handover.

Client feedback delays, missing assets, and third-party approvals can
affect the timeline.

## 5. Exclusions

-   Domain, hosting, email, and renewals.
-   Paid image licenses and paid third-party services.
-   Custom CMS or admin dashboard.
-   Database-backed CRM and advanced lead assignment.
-   Lender, insurance, mutual-fund, or stock-market APIs.
-   Customer accounts, payments, loan applications, or document uploads.
-   Legal/regulatory review, certification, and compliance guarantees.
-   Copywriting in additional languages.
-   Ongoing maintenance, marketing, and monthly SEO campaigns.

## 6. Change control

Any feature not listed in the approved scope should be documented as a
change request with revised cost and timeline before work begins.

## 7. Handover

-   Source repository and agreed ownership/access.
-   Production deployment.
-   Basic instructions for updating content and managing configuration.
-   Known limitations and third-party account list.
-   Agreed warranty/support terms.

## 8. Warranty recommendation

Specify a short post-launch defect-fix period in the contract, for
example 15--30 days for defects against the approved scope. New
features, new content, third-party outages, and changes requested after
approval are not defect fixes.
